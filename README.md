The Hidden Fragility of National Economies
An Explainable AI and Machine Learning Approach to Trade Dependence and Economic Vulnerability
Overview
This project investigates economic vulnerability across national economies by combining international trade data with macroeconomic and fiscal indicators.
It introduces an Economic Fragility Index (EFI) and applies Machine Learning and Explainable AI (SHAP) to classify and analyze economic fragility across countries and over time.
Study Period: 2010–2024
Countries: 81
Valid EFI Observations: 714
Methodology
The Economic Fragility Index is constructed using three dimensions:
* Trade Vulnerability: Export partner and product concentration.
* Macroeconomic Instability: GDP growth volatility, inflation, and unemployment.
* Fiscal Vulnerability: Public debt and fiscal deficit.
Countries are classified into four categories:
1. Low Fragility
2. Moderate Fragility
3. High Fragility
4. Critical Fragility
Six supervised Machine Learning algorithms are evaluated, followed by SHAP analysis to interpret model predictions and identify influential economic indicators.

Research Contribution
This project demonstrates how Economics, Mathematics, Data Science, Machine Learning, and Explainable Artificial Intelligence can be integrated to investigate trade dependence and economic vulnerability.
The proposed EFI provides a comparative analytical framework rather than a universal measure of national economic stability.
